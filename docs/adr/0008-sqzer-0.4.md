# ADR-0008: The page on `sqzer` 0.4.0

**Status:** Accepted   **Date:** 2026-10-28   **Deciders:** Vlad (sole maintainer)
**Scope:** What the page takes from `sqzer` 0.4.0: the defaults from `options()` in place of the page's copy, the size of each trial in the list, the toast and the chart, a result lossy at no quality, the package's refusals at this version, and the notices of the crates in its module. ADR-0007 D6 kept the defaults the README stated in `lib/defaults.ts` and showed `default` with no number where it stated none; D1 here replaces that. ADR-0007 D3's untouched target slider changes only before a worker has loaded. ADR-0001 D4's chart gains a third line. Nothing else in ADR-0001 to ADR-0007 changes.

---

## 1. Context

The page asked three things of the package while ADR-0001 items 7 to 9 were built, and 0.4.0 answers all three:

```text
sqzer#57    options() lists the keys optimize and encode take, each with its default as the
            package spells it and its help. A key whose default depends on the image says auto,
            one that does nothing when left out has none
sqzer#55    every Trial, in trials and in what onTrial receives, carries size, the bytes it
            encoded to
sqzer#56    an explicit quality with no format, on a graphic the portable tier sends to lossless
            WebP, is reported lossless: true. A quality with a format whose encoder in this build
            is lossless only is an error: EncoderUnavailable naming native-webp for WebP,
            Unsupported for PNG
```

The release also adds `png:colors` and `png:dither`, a palette through `quantizr`, which `codecs()` lists with the other backend options, and ships `THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.txt`, the notice of every crate compiled into `sqzer_bg.wasm`, for a page that bundles the package to ship alongside.

What 0.4.0 does with what the page could send, tried in Node against the published package:

```text
lossless with png:colors           InvalidParams: a lossless target contradicts png:colors=16,
or png:optimize_alpha              which gives samples up. The page leaves it to the package
quality with PNG                   Unsupported. The page never sends it: the quality shows only
                                   where codecs() says the encoder is lossy
quality with WebP                  EncoderUnavailable, available in native-webp. Never sent, as above
png:colors                         lossless: false, no quality, no search, and target: 70, the
                                   default of a search that did not run
```

---

## 2. Decision

### D1. The defaults are what `options()` lists, carried by the worker's `ready`

The worker answers its load with `options()` beside `codecs()`. The encoder actor turns the listing into `Defaults`, each key with its default spelled as the package spells it, `70`, `inside`, `auto`, a key with none left out, and hands that to the machine with the codecs. `lib/defaults.ts` holds the type and the reading of it, and no default of its own.

A control shows the package's default from there until it is touched, as ADR-0007 D6 has it, and sends nothing. What changes is what is known:

```text
effort        rests at 6, said `default: 6`. It was a notch that said `default: the package's`
background    the empty field shows `auto` as its placeholder, where it said `default`
target        rests at 70 from options(), where 70 was the README's number
max pixels    24000000, from options()
fit, position, filter, subsampling
              the select opens on what options() says
chips         every option sent says its default, `effort 9, default 6`, and a value equal to
              the default, chosen by hand, is no chip, a switch turned back off included
alert         TooLarge names the limit the read ran under, the reader's or the package's
```

The worker loads as the page opens, before any image, so the controls have the defaults by the time a file is picked. If they do not, a control says `default` with no number, a select opens on nothing and says `default`, and the target slider rests at the `web` mark, which is ADR-0007 D3 with the README's number gone.

### D2. The size of each trial is in the list, the toast and the chart

The trial list gains a third column, the size each trial encoded to, as `bytes()` prints it, `48.2 KB`. The search toast says it after the score: `Trial 3 of at most 8: quality 62 scores 69.1 at 48.2 KB.`

The chart draws the sizes of the current run as a third line, dotted, on the rules the score and the quality share: each size a share of the input's, so the top rule is the input and a line near the bottom is a file that came out small. The last size is written at the line's end, over it, or under it where the line comes down into the point, so the number is off the line; a run of one trial gets a point of its own, since one point draws no line. The target's label keeps clear of the line and the number, as it does of the other two lines. A size past the input's stops at the top rule, as a score past 100 would. The runs before keep their two lines and no size: each was a share of its own input, and the picked image may have changed since.

```text
the maintainer's pick of six drafts, 2026-10-28: the third line against the input, without a label
naming the input at the top rule
rejected    a line from 0 to the largest trial, which makes a spread of a few kB look steep; the
            points sized by the bytes; a faint bar per trial; a strip of its own under the chart
```

### D3. A result lossy at no quality says so, and no target

An encode with `png:colors` is lossy, has no quality, and searched nothing, and the record still says `target: 70`. The details say `quality lossy`, as the command line's `{quality}` does, and leave the target line out; the chart draws no rule for it. A target is told of only where the result has a quality, which a search and `fast` both report. The page prints the record as it comes; whether `target` belongs in that record is a question for `sqzer`.

### D4. The package's refusals stay the package's

Nothing new is checked on the page. Lossless with an option that gives samples up is `InvalidParams`, the alert of ADR-0001 D6 with the package's sentence under it, as ADR-0007 has it for `cover` with one side.

### D5. The notices of the crates go into `licenses.txt`

`THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.txt` joins the package's two licence texts in the entry for `sqzer` that the build appends to `licenses.txt`, so the page ships the notice of every crate the worker runs, as most of those licences ask. The file grows from about 58 kB to about 282 kB, and is fetched only by a reader who follows the footer's link.

---

## 3. Options considered

**Call `options()` on the page.** The listing does not depend on an image, and a call on the page would have it before the worker loads. Rejected: every call into `sqzer` runs in the worker, and the page would load the module a second time for a list the worker sends as it loads.

**Keep `lib/defaults.ts` as the value until the worker loads.** Rejected: two copies of the defaults, which is what sqzer#57 asked to be rid of, and a version bump could leave them apart for the moment the page shows the stale one.

**The size on a scale of its own.** From 0 to the largest trial, as a line, as bars behind the lines, or as a strip under the chart; or the points of the score sized by the bytes. Drafted and rejected in favour of the input's scale (D2): the share of the input is what the reader came for, and a scale of the trials' own range turns a difference of a few kB into a cliff.

---

## 4. Trade-offs

**No number before the load.** For the moment between the page opening and the worker loading, a control that would show a default says only `default`. A reader rarely sees it: an image cannot be picked much faster than the module loads.

**A larger licence file.** About 224 kB of notices, static and fetched on request.

---

## 5. Consequences

- ADR-0007 gets a `Superseded by` line for the defaults of D6.
- `CLAUDE.md` says the defaults come from `options()`, and where the untouched target slider stands. The `codecs-drive-the-controls` rule in `.greptile/config.json` says the same.
- A version bump of `sqzer` no longer reads the README for defaults. It still reads the release notes for what the page shows of a result.
- `pnpm-workspace.yaml` exempts `sqzer@0.4.0` from pnpm's one-day release age, which pnpm wrote at `pnpm install` on the day of the release. It does nothing once the day has passed.
